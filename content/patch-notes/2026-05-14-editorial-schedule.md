---
title: A real editorial calendar
date: 2026-05-14
category: Site
---
For a long time I ran the site on whenever-I-feel-like-it energy. Three pieces in a weekend, then two weeks of nothing. The four-week schedule isn't strict, but having one forces me to think about pacing instead of just output.

Drafts now move through outline, draft, edit, and scheduled. I can see four weeks of articles laid out ahead, which means I can slot ideas where they fit instead of wedging them in whenever I happen to write them.
