title: The three suits, dialed in
date: 2026-03-23
category: Design
---
Spent a day on a real palette pass for each theme. Miles is black and red with crisp white type, pulled from the Brooklyn suit. Peter went dark red with a blue accent, his classic colorway inverted so red dominates the page. Venom is near-black with pure white accents and the cold border tones you'd expect.

Themes change more than colors. Borders, type weight, and the cursor all shift between modes.
