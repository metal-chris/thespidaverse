The longer reviews were getting cramped in the old layout, so I added some structural pieces:

- A sticky table of contents on desktop, collapsed into a toggle on mobile
- Drop caps on opening paragraphs
- Pullquotes that interrupt the column for emphasis
- A related-articles section at the bottom, sorted by shared tags and falling back to category

The drop cap is the only purely decorative one. The rest came out of me scrolling through my own writing trying to find specific sections and getting annoyed.
