The Spidaverse went live, technically. Public-facing it was a coming-soon landing page, but behind the access gate were the three character themes (Miles, Peter, Venom) and the first version of The Web, a D3 graph linking every piece of media on the site through shared tags and categories.

The Web was the original idea for the project. Pop culture functions as one big interconnected thing, not six separate verticals, and I wanted a section that actually visualized that. So it was the first thing I built.
