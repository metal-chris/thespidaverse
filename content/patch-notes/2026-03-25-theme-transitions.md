---
title: Theme transitions, in character
date: 2026-03-25
category: Design
---
Theme switching used to be an instant repaint, which felt cheap. Each character now has its own transition: Miles uses a graffiti-stroke wipe, Peter does a comic-panel reveal, Venom hits the page with a black symbiote sweep that drains away.

There's a toggle in the footer if you'd rather it just snap.
