The content model finally got organized. There are now eight categories: Movies, TV, Video Games, Anime, Books, Music, Culture, Tech. And twelve recurring formats that every article picks from: First Bite, The Full Web, Spin the Block, The Sinister Six, The Gauntlet, Versus, The Daily Bugle, Spida Sense, The Web Sling, State of the Game, The Rotation, One Year Later.

Categories cover the subject. Formats handle structure, which is more useful than it sounds: a First Bite is two paragraphs of first impressions, a Sinister Six is a ranked list, and they aren't interchangeable even when they're about the same movie.
