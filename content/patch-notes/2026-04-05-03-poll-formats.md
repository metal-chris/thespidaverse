title: Three new ways to weigh in
date: 2026-04-05
category: Feature
---
Added three poll formats for review pages, and now I pick the one that fits the piece:

- This or That, for binary takes
- Ranking, where you drag items into your preferred order
- Hot Take Meter, a slider asking how spicy my opinion was

The generic 1-5 widget wasn't producing useful data and the prompt was always vague. These at least ask something specific.
