The Currently Consuming strip on the homepage now pulls from real services. Spotify for music, Trakt for movies and TV, Steam for games, AniList for anime.

Before this I was updating it by hand, which meant I'd forget to do it for two weeks and then everyone would think I was watching nothing. Now if I'm watching nothing, the strip stays empty.
