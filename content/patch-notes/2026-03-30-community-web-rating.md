date: 2026-03-30

Added a community rating slider to every review. 1 to 100, one vote per reader, average updates live. Built on top of the analytics dashboard and poll backend I'd been wiring up the same week.

I'm not pretending the community average is some objective number, but it does tell me when I scored something completely differently from how the room read it. That gap is usually where the interesting conversation is.
