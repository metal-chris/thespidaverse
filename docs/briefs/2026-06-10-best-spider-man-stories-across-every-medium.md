## Suggested angles

### 1. The definitive six, one per medium
Lean into the format fully: lock in exactly six picks, one from each distinct medium (comics, film, animation, live-action TV, video games, prose/other). The argument isn't "here are six great Spider-Man things" — it's that each medium has produced at least one story that could only exist in that form. Comics: *Kraven's Last Hunt* (or *Life Story* if you want something you can hand someone today). Film: *Into the Spider-Verse* (the aesthetic breakthrough that redefined what superhero animation can be). Animation: *The Spectacular Spider-Man* 2008 series (tighter than any animated series before or since). Live-action TV: *Spider-Noir* as the fresh wildcard pick — only works as pulp noir, only works as TV. Games: Insomniac's *Marvel's Spider-Man* (the web-swinging as the storytelling). And comics again for the wild-card sixth — Hickman's *Ultimate Spider-Man* as the newest addition to the canon. The ranking poll then asks readers: which medium got Spider-Man *most* right?

### 2. What makes a Spider-Man story essential — and does Brand New Day have it?
Use the list as a lens. Every story that makes the cut shares a core: Peter sacrifices something real, the cost is human (not cosmic), and the villain functions as a mirror. *Kraven's Last Hunt* — identity. *Into the Spider-Verse* — responsibility reframed. *Life Story* — time. Set that thesis up, then use it to preview what *Brand New Day* needs to do (Peter's been erased from the world — the amnesia is the mirror) and whether it's on track. The ranking poll doubles as reader sentiment on Brand New Day's setup.

### 3. The recency bias trap — why the best Spidey stories might not be the obvious ones
Everyone's going to say *Into the Spider-Verse*. The interesting piece is what gets unfairly left off the list. Build the six around underseen or under-discussed picks: *Spider-Man: Life Story* over the entire Nick Spencer run, *Spectacular Spider-Man* (2008) over *Ultimate Spider-Man* (2012 Disney), Hickman's *Ultimate* over Bendis's, the PS4 game's story over its sequel. Make the case that the obvious choices aren't always the correct ones, and use the poll to let the audience push back.
