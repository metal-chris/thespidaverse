## Suggested angles

### 1. The phantom launch — grieving the game that should already be here
This piece could lean into the uncanny feeling of sitting in what was supposed to be a post-GTA 6 world. The original Fall 2025 date meant that by June 2026, we'd have had six months of memes, speedruns, GTA Online 2.0 discourse, and a functioning community around the new map. Instead, we're sitting in a content desert, hyping ourselves up for a trailer that doesn't exist yet. The angle is half-eulogy, half-anticipation: what the community has been doing to fill the void (endless speculation, Rockstar archaeology, GTA Online farewell tours), and whether the wait has changed what we expect the game to be. Works well as a `the-full-web`-style deep take because it invites a web rating on the *anticipation itself* — where does hype sit right now, 0–100?

### 2. GTA Online's final summer — what we're actually saying goodbye to
The likely-final GTA Online summer DLC makes the handoff to GTA 6 feel concrete in a way the November release date doesn't. This angle focuses on GTA Online as a 13-year live-service miracle: $1.32M/day, 10M weekly players, still running on hardware that launched when Obama was president. The piece would audit what GTA Online built — the economy, the culture, the meme-tier grind — and ask what GTA 6 Online has to match. Not a nostalgia piece, but a bar-setting one. The `agree_scale` poll maps perfectly: "GTA Online is irreplaceable — agree or disagree?"

### 3. The most expensive summer marketing campaign in gaming history
With Trailer 3, pricing, and pre-orders all landing in the same late-June window, Rockstar is about to fire the biggest marketing salvo since, arguably, GTA V in 2013. This angle unpacks what's at stake: Take-Two has guided $8–8.2B in FY2027 bookings almost entirely on GTA 6's back; any wobble in the campaign read has real financial consequences. The piece would put the marketing stakes in context — what Rockstar *can't* afford to get wrong, what "doing it differently from GTA V" might actually mean (no E3? Exclusive platform deals? A Beyoncé?), and why every major publisher cleared their summer slate to avoid the blast radius.

- What was the *original* Fall 2025 target date? Was a specific date ever given or just a window? Needed to nail the "six months in" math in the piece.
- Any confirmed special editions, bundles, or pricing tiers for GTA 6? Zelnick said pricing announces with marketing — watch Take-Two's investor comms and Rockstar Newswire closely this week.
- Is GTA 6 confirmed for PC at launch, or still console-only at release? This affects who the audience is and the framing of "day one" discussions.
