## Suggested angles

**Angle 1 — The Three-Platform Problem**
Bleach is on Hulu/Disney+, Ghost in the Shell is on Prime Video, Elusive Samurai is on Crunchyroll. That's three different subscriptions to follow the three biggest shows of the season. The piece could open with that friction as a hook — "summer 2026 is going to cost you" — and turn it into a quick breakdown of which platform you *have* to have this season and which ones justify the spend. It keeps the Bugle voice punchy and practical rather than just another hype list.

**Angle 2 — The Comeback/Finale/Reboot Trifecta**
Each of the three shows represents a distinct cultural moment: Bleach is a *finale* (13 years from manga end to anime end), Elusive Samurai is a *comeback* (fan favorite getting its real arc), Ghost in the Shell is a *reboot* (Science SARU reinventing a landmark IP). The article could frame the summer as a season about legacies — what happens when anime finally catches up with stories fans have been waiting on. This angle gives the piece a unifying thesis rather than just three capsule previews.

**Angle 3 — Pick Your Lane (The Poll Setup)**
Lean directly into the multiple_choice poll format: structure the whole piece around asking the reader which of the three they're most hyped for, and give each show a 1-paragraph stinger that makes the case for it. No hedging, no "all three are great" — Spida-Mane takes a side (probably Ghost in the Shell as the surprise wildcard pick) and invites readers to argue back. This is the most engagement-optimized angle and maps cleanly onto the poll structure.
